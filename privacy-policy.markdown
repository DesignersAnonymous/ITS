### 1. Who we are

In The Stix Boutique Retreat & Spa ("In The Stix", "we", "us" or "our") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

We understand that when you share your personal information with us, you want to know that it will be treated responsibly, stored securely and only used for legitimate purposes.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, who we may share it with and the rights you have in relation to your information.

For the purposes of applicable UK data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, the data controller is:

**Rutland Glamping UK LLP trading as In The Stix Boutique Retreat & Spa**

Registered address: Brook Farm, Wood Lane, Greetham, Rutland, LE15 7SN

Website: https://inthestix.co.uk

Contact email: hello@inthestix.co.uk

### 2. Information we collect

We may collect and process the following types of personal information when you visit our website, contact us, make a booking or use our facilities and services.

**Personal and contact information**
- Full name
- Email address
- Telephone number
- Home or billing address

**Booking and guest information**
- Booking dates and accommodation preferences
- Number of guests and relevant guest details
- Booking history and additional purchases
- Special requests or information you voluntarily provide to help us prepare for your stay
- Communications between you and our team

**Payment information**
- Payment confirmations and transaction details
- Billing information
- Details of purchases and refunds

Payments are processed securely through third-party payment providers such as Stripe. We do not routinely receive or store your full payment card details.

**Spa and wellness information**

If you book one of our spa experiences or treatments, you may choose or be asked to provide relevant information such as allergies, sensitivities, accessibility requirements or other information necessary to help us provide a safe and appropriate experience.

Where this information amounts to special category personal data, we will only process it where we have an appropriate lawful basis and an applicable condition under data protection law.

**Website and technical information**

When you visit our website, we may collect:
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- Website pages visited
- Time spent on pages
- Referral sources and website interaction information

This information helps us understand how visitors use our website and improve the experience.

**Managing bookings and reservations**
- Processing and confirming accommodation and spa bookings.
- Sending booking confirmations and important updates.
- Managing payments, deposits, cancellations and refunds.
- Responding to questions and special requests.

- Responding to enquiries.

- Analysing website traffic and visitor behaviour.
- Understanding which services and experiences are popular.
- Improving our website, facilities and services.

### 5. Our lawful basis for processing your information

We will only process your personal information where we have a valid legal basis to do so.

These may include:

**Contractual necessity**

Where we need your information to fulfil a booking, process a payment, provide accommodation or deliver a service you have requested.

**Legal obligations**

Where we are required to retain or disclose information to comply with applicable laws, accounting requirements, tax obligations or other legal duties.

**Legitimate interests**

Where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided these interests do not override your rights and freedoms.

Examples include improving our services, managing our business, responding to enquiries and protecting our website and facilities.

**Consent**

Where you have given us permission to use your information for a specific purpose, such as receiving marketing emails or processing certain sensitive information.

You can withdraw your consent at any time.

### 6. Marketing communications

We would love to keep in touch and let you know about what's happening at In The Stix.

We may send you information about our accommodation, spa experiences, retreats, events and special offers where we have your consent or another lawful permission to do so.

We use Mailchimp to manage and deliver some of our email marketing communications.

Mailchimp may collect information about whether emails are opened and which links are clicked. We use this information to understand engagement and improve our communications, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of our emails.

We will not sell your personal information or pass your details to other businesses for their own marketing purposes without your permission.

### 7. Who we share your information with

We respect your privacy and only share personal information where necessary to operate our business, provide our services or meet our legal obligations.

We may share relevant information with trusted third-party providers, including:

- **SuperSaaS:** to manage relevant bookings and reservations.
- **Stripe:** to process payments and refunds.
- **Mailchimp:** to manage email marketing communications.
- **Website hosting and technical providers:** to maintain and operate our website.
- **Analytics providers:** to help us understand website performance and visitor behaviour.
- **Professional advisers:** including accountants, insurers and legal advisers where necessary.
- **Regulatory authorities:** where we are legally required to provide information.

These organisations may only use your information in accordance with their applicable responsibilities and contractual arrangements.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

### 8. How long we keep your information

We only retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

Retention periods will depend on the type of information and the reason we hold it.

For example:
- Booking and financial records may be retained for the periods required by tax and accounting legislation.
- Enquiry information may be retained for a reasonable period to manage and follow up on your enquiry.
- Marketing information will be retained until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent, subject to any necessary suppression records.
- Spa and wellness information will only be retained for as long as necessary for the relevant service, safety and legal purposes.

When information is no longer required, we will securely delete it or anonymise it.

### 9. Cookies and website tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors use our website.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

They may be used for the following purposes:

**Essential cookies**

These are necessary for the website to function properly, including security and basic website functionality.

**Analytics and performance cookies**

These help us understand how visitors use our website, which pages are popular and how we can improve the website.

**Marketing and advertising cookies**

Where used, these may help us understand the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and provide more relevant marketing.

Non-essential cookies will only be used where the required consent has been obtained.

You can manage your cookie preferences through the cookie settings available on our website. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Please be aware that disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

### 10. Keeping your information secure

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against accidental loss, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

Access to personal information is restricted to those who need it for legitimate business purposes.

Where we use third-party providers, we take reasonable steps to ensure they have appropriate security measures and contractual protections in place.

However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing electronic information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

### 11. International transfers of information

Some of the third-party providers we use may store or process personal information outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation.

These safeguards may include UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or other legally recognised transfer mechanisms.

You can contact us if you would like further information about international transfers and the safeguards we use.

### 12. Your rights

Under applicable data protection legislation, you have a number of rights concerning your personal information.

These include:

**The right to access**

You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

**The right to rectification**

You can ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.

**The right to erasure**

You can ask us to delete your personal information in certain circumstances.

**The right to restrict processing**

You can ask us to restrict how we use your information in certain circumstances.

**The right to data portability**

You may request certain personal information in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.

**The right to object**

You can object to certain types of processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests.

You have an absolute right to object to your personal information being used for direct marketing.

**The right to withdraw consent**

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in section 1.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

We will normally respond within one month, subject to any lawful extension.
