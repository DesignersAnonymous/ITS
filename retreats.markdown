  Whether you’re hosting a wellness retreat, creative workshop, corporate escape or intimate group gathering, In The Stix offers a unique luxury countryside venue designed to bring people together.

  Set within peaceful rural surroundings beneath the Rutland skies, our boutique glamping retreat provides the perfect balance of comfort, nature and privacy. With luxury domes, wood-fired hot tubs, communal gathering spaces and open outdoor areas, guests can fully switch off from the distractions of everyday life and immerse themselves in the experience you create.

  Ideal for wellness leaders, yoga instructors, coaches, creatives and brands looking to host meaningful retreats, In The Stix creates an inspiring backdrop for connection, relaxation and memorable shared experiences.

  From sunrise yoga and breathwork sessions to fireside dining, team retreats and celebration weekends, we offer a flexible and atmospheric space where your retreat can truly stand out.

  A place to gather. Space to breathe. Experiences your guests will remember long after they leave.
